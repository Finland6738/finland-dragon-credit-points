# Quick Start: Officers and Guild Master

> **Built, not yet seen in the game.** [Italiano](Quick-Start-Officer-it)

## Once, at the start of a season (Guild Master)

1. In **Settings › Rules** choose a preset or edit the rules, then **Save** ([[Settings|Settings]]). Only the Guild Master changes rules and the permissions table.
2. In **Settings › Season** open the season: its pack of 10, 20 or 40 raids ([[Seasons|Seasons]]).
3. Officers fill the pool: players, declarations, main and alt links ([[Roster and Nights|Roster-and-Nights]]).

## Every raid night

1. **Open the night** in **Roster › Night** and check the lineup and the bench. [[Addon Check|Addon-Check]] shows who lacks the addon; notify them in `/raid`.
2. **Master looter:** set the loot method to Master Looter. After each kill the session opens on **Loot › Session**: check the list, **Start the session**, then **Confirm**, **Reject** or **Modify** (with a reason) each proposal ([[Master Looter Guide|Master-Looter-Guide]]). What master loot cannot hand over waits in **To deliver**.
3. **Council:** votes, cancelling an award and correcting credits, always with a reason, from **Loot › Council** or `/fdcp council` ([[Council|Council]]). Legendary projects: `/fdcp legendary`. BoE and the guild bank: `/fdcp bank`.
4. **Close the night.** Confirm the proposed absence penalties; the addon saves the site files.

## Afterwards

- Read what happened in [[History|History]]; share the table with [[Export|Export]] (Discord, CSV, JSON).
- Update the community site: [[Site Export|Site-Export]].
- Back up the ledger regularly and compact closed seasons: [[Backup and Seasons|Backup-and-Seasons]].
- If officers' tables differ, wait for the sync and check the **Sync** line of `/fdcp debug` ([[Commands|Commands]]).

The rules in short: [[Loot Rules|Loot-Rules]]. Questions: [[FAQ|FAQ]].
