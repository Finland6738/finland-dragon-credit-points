# Quick Start: Raider

> **Built, not yet seen in the game.** [Italiano](Quick-Start-Raider-it)

1. **Install** the addon ([[Installation|Installation]]) and log in. Everyone in the raid needs it: only players with an active, compatible addon can roll.
2. **Look at your place.** `/fdcp` opens the window; the **Credits** tab shows the season's table and your place in the queue. Fewer credits means more priority ([[Credits Table|Credits]]).
3. **Be in the roster.** An officer adds you to the pool and your declaration (main spec, off-spec, role) is locked for the season: [[Roster and Nights|Roster-and-Nights]], [[Loot Rules|Loot-Rules]].
4. **When a boss dies**, a popup shows each item with its cost, the timer, your credits and the **upgrade badge** ([[Upgrade Hints|Upgrade-Hints]]).
5. **Press one of three buttons:** **Pass**, **Roll Main-spec** or **Roll Off-spec**. A roll button answers and rolls in one click. A `/roll` typed by hand does not count ([[Rolling for Loot|Rolling-for-Loot]]).
6. **Wait for the proposal.** The master looter confirms it; the result and the reason are written in `/raid`. The winner pays the item's cost in credits.
7. **Do not cheat the roll:** changing gear after the fight or hiding an upgrade costs +2 credits ([[Loot Rules|Loot-Rules]]).
