# Reading the Credits Table

> **Built, not yet seen in the game.** The tab works in the offline tests with a simulated client; how it looks in the real game is still to be confirmed. [Italiano](Credits-it)

The **Credits** tab is the guild's loot credits table, as on the community site. Credits are never stored: they are worked out from the ledger every time, so every client that has the same events shows the same numbers. **Fewer credits means more priority.**

## One accordion per season

Each season is a card with its name, the number of nights, whether it is in progress or closed, the date of the last night and how many players are in the roster. The newest season is open; click another season's header to open it (the one that was open folds).

## The table

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Queue** | Your place in the loot queue: the lowest total first, then the name. |
| **Player** | The name in the class colour (with a square). |
| **Class · Spec** | The class and the declared main spec. |
| One column per night | The date and the instance of the night, and `+N` for what the player paid for items won that night; a dot when nothing. A player who won two items in a night shows the sum. The newest 8 nights are shown; older ones are still counted in the total. |
| **Total** | The ledger's total in a chip: the initial credits of the season (late joiners start with the roster average), plus every award and officer adjustment, minus the awards that were cancelled. |

Because of the initial credits and the adjustments, the total can be more than the sum of the night columns. An award belongs to the night that was open when it was recorded.

## Filters, sorting, your row

- **All / Tank / Healer / DPS** and the **Class** button (click it to step through the classes, and back to all) filter the rows. The queue place does not change: it is always the place in the whole queue.
- The **Horde / Alliance** switch picks the faction's queue; it starts on your own.
- Click a header to sort by it, again to reverse.
- Your own row (or the row of your main, when you play an alt) is highlighted with an amber bar.

Scroll a long table with the mouse wheel.
