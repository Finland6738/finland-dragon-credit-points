### Added

- M5-4: council flags. New pure `Domain/Flags.lua` (analysis §5.2, "Come è fatto (M5-4)"):
  - **Upgrade ignored.** A raider who may roll the item (present, with the addon) **passes** on an item that is a **big upgrade for their main spec** (MS1). A player who has not answered is flagged only when a verdict of theirs is known (today a verdict arrives only with an answer). An off-spec or transmog answer is not a pass.
  - **Possible waste.** The first in line of the full-cost tier (MS) has a **small upgrade or none**, another candidate of the same tier a **big** one at least `council.wasteRatio` times larger (preset 2); the candidate with the most to gain is named. Before the proposal the line follows the frozen credits and the first valid roll; once the proposal is written, the first is the rules' winner (no waste is said when the item went to the off-spec tier, the bank, disenchant or the master looter).
  - **Main spec only, thresholds checked again.** Only the main-spec verdict (`m`) is read, never the off-spec one; "big" is checked again against the guild's `upgrade` thresholds (stats or item level), so a BIG under the threshold raises nothing; an unusable item or a damaged verdict never counts.
  - **In the session.** `SessionFSM.Flags` computes them from the item; the master looter sees them under the candidates **as soon as the answers arrive**, before the round closes, and in the *Notes* column of the player they name (*Upgrade ignored: Bryn passed on it (Big upgrade +12.0%).*). With the proposal their codes join `proposal.flags`, so they reach the `ITEM_AWARDED` event and the raid's view; the details stay on the item. **They never change the winner, the cost or the order**; they are not informational flags, so an `auto` award waits for the master looter. Acting on them is a Modify with its reason (*Obvious waste*, *Passed to game the system*). The session now freezes the rules' `upgrade` and `council` sections too (an older saved session uses the presets).
  - **Rules.** New keys in the `council` section: `flags` (on), `wasteRatio` (2, from 1 to 100) and `verdicts` = `council` (preset) | `officers` | `ml`: who sees the verdicts and the flags besides the master looter, who always does. Rules written before are filled with the presets.
  - **Verdicts visible only to the council.** On another client the read-only window shows the candidates (answers, labels, notes, the Upgrade column; no credits and no rolls) and the flags only where `council.verdicts` allows it; a council member (`council.member`) now gets the read-only window even without `award.confirm`. Everybody else sees only the codes of the proposal, without names. Known limit: the answers travel on the raid channel (M5-2), so the visibility is the window's, not the data's.
  - Words: `Reasons` group `FLAG` (`UPGRADE_IGNORED`, `POSSIBLE_WASTE`) and the `ML_FLAG_*` keys, in English and Italian. Documentation: wiki *Master Looter Guide* (new section "Council flags"), *Loot Rules* and *Rolling for Loot*, in English and Italian; README.
  - Tests: new `Tests/test_flags.lua` (both flags on the documented cases, never on MS2 or below the thresholds, the ratio, the line, the settings, the state machine, the auto award, the view's visibility), `Tests/test_ui_ml_session.lua` extended.
  - **Not verified in the game:** all of it runs against the mock client only. How the flag lines sit in the strip of the master looter's window in a real raid (length, wrapping), and the read-only window of a council member who is not an officer.
- M5-3: personal stat weights, the Pawn-like import and the best-in-slot wishlist (analysis §5.1 points 3 and 5, "Come è fatto (M5-3)"). New pure `Domain/WeightImport.lua` and `Domain/Wishlist.lua`, new `App/PersonalUpgrade.lua`, the commands `/fdcp weights` and `/fdcp wish`:
  - **Weight import.** `/fdcp weights import <spec> <string>` reads a string in a Pawn-like format (`( Pawn: v1: "Name": Strength=1, CritRating=0.85 )`, or the bare `Strength=1, Agility=0.5`, or the addon's short codes); `<spec>` is a spec id or `ms` / `os` (the declared specs). A valid string replaces that spec's weights as a whole and turns the personal weights on; an invalid one is refused with a message and changes nothing (empty, too long, not `Stat=number`, a number that is not a plain decimal, no stat the score knows). Stats the score does not know (sockets, gems) are listed and ignored. The format and the stat names are the public ones; no code of any other addon is used. `show` prints the weights of a spec as a string (the personal ones, else the defaults), `off` / `on` switch the personal weights without losing them, `reset <spec | all>` removes them.
  - **Off by default and reversible (issue #2 stays open).** Nothing is stored until the player imports; the default weights of M5-1 apply otherwise. The weights are personal (`FinlandDragonCreditPointsCharDB.upgrade`), never in the ledger or the shared data, and the guild's rules are not asked: the verdict only informs the player and the council and never changes who wins. There are no guild-wide BiS lists.
  - **Feeding the advisor.** `UpgradeAdvisor.For` passes the personal row of the main-spec / off-spec to the score when the personal weights are on, and the popup's tooltip says "Your own stat weights were used". The verdict that goes to the master looter is the one computed with them.
  - **Wishlist.** `/fdcp wish add <item link or id> ...`, `remove`, `list`, `clear`. An item on the list carries an **On your BiS list** badge above the verdict lines in the popup (with a tooltip), and the popup follows the list while it is open. It is saved per character, survives `/reload`, is cleaned when read (damaged entries dropped), holds at most 300 items and is never sent to anyone.
  - Documentation: new wiki page *Upgrade Hints* (English and Italian), the *Commands* page, the *Rolling for Loot* page and the *Home* and sidebar links.
  - **Not verified in the game:** all of it runs against the mock client only. How the new badge row sits in the popup in a real raid, that shift-clicking an item link into the `/fdcp wish` line works in the chat box as assumed, and whether the stat names of real Pawn strings map as expected (the names used are the commonly published ones; the stats the addon cannot read in the game, spike S5, stay out of reach of any weight).
- M5-2: inventory adapter and upgrade verdicts in the popup. New `Adapters/Inventory.lua`, `App/UpgradeAdvisor.lua`, a rules section `upgrade`, and an extension of the session protocol (analysis §5.1, "Come è fatto (M5-2)"):
  - **Inventory adapter.** Reads the worn pieces by slot (`GetInventoryItemLink`; shirt and tabard are not compared), an item's data (`C_Item.GetItemInfo` / `GetItemInfoInstant`, `GetDetailedItemLevelInfo`, `GetItemStats`, `IsEquippableItem`) and its specs (`GetItemSpecInfo`, else `DoesItemContainSpec` over the class), all through `Core/Compat.lua`. When `GetItemStats` gives nothing on one side, `GetItemStatDelta` fills it in; a secret value reads as "no stats".
  - **Verdicts per spec.** `UpgradeAdvisor.For(itemID)` computes the score twice: with the weights of the declared **MS1** (Main-spec) and of the **MS2** (Off-spec); without a declaration the spec being played is the main-spec and there is no off-spec line. The result is kept until the equipment, the spec, the ledger or the rules change.
  - **The popup** shows one line per spec under the cost chip (*Main-spec · Big upgrade +7.4% · ilvl +6*, green / accent / neutral / amber, decimal comma in Italian) with a tooltip: why, what the comparison used (stats or item level only), what is worn, and the limits. The words are locale keys and new `Reasons` groups `VERDICT` and `UPGRADE`, in English and Italian.
  - **Unusable item → Pass preselected** (rule `upgrade.autoPass`, on by default): only when every computed verdict says the character cannot use the item; the popup shows Pass selected and a help line, and **sends nothing by itself** (no answer counts as a pass); clicking Pass sends it. A token or anything "not comparable" never passes by itself.
  - **Rules section `upgrade`** (`enabled`, `autoPass`, `useStats`, `bigThreshold`, `smallThreshold`, `ilvlBigThreshold`), validated with the rest of the rules and filled in with the defaults for rules written before this step.
  - **Protocol version 3** (`pm` stays 2: a client of protocol 2 is understood and understands). `SRESP` carries optional `vd` (verdicts for main-spec and off-spec) and `eq` (up to four `item:…` strings of the worn pieces) with every answer, passes included; a damaged field is dropped and the response still counts. The state machine keeps them per player (`item.verdicts`, `item.equipped`); the master looter's candidates table has a sortable **Upgrade** column and a row tooltip with both verdicts and the worn pieces.
  - Documentation: the wiki pages *Rolling for Loot* ("Is it an upgrade?" and its limits), *Master Looter Guide* (the Upgrade column) and *Loot Rules* (the new words), in English and Italian.
  - **Not verified in the game:** all of it runs against the mock client only. Whether `GetItemStats`, `GetItemStatDelta`, `GetDetailedItemLevelInfo` and `GetItemSpecInfo` answer for real raid items on WoW Forever as the addon assumes (spike S5), whether `GetItemSpecInfo` lists the specs as assumed (otherwise a usable piece might be preselected as Pass; it is only a preselection), the item level of a worn piece with upgrades, the popup's layout with two verdict lines in a real raid, and the tooltips. Known limit, by design: the comparison starts from the item id, so a drop with bonuses is read as its base item.
- M5-1: upgrade score. New pure `Domain/UpgradeScore.lua` and the data file `Data/StatWeights.lua` (analysis §5.1, "Come è fatto (M5-1)"):
  - **Can the character use it.** A proficiency table per class (armour types, shields, librams / idols / totems, weapon types, wands, dual wield; the enhancement shaman's dual wield is per spec), editable through the configuration; the item's class and spec restrictions when the adapter knows them. An unusable item is *no upgrade* with `usable = false` and a code (the popup will preselect Pass, M5-2); a token or another non-equippable item is *not comparable* and never refused by itself.
  - **Against what.** Rings and trinkets against the worse of the two equipped (an empty slot first, the same item already worn against itself, an equipped use-effect trinket kept); a two-hander against main hand + off hand (stats summed, item levels averaged); a one-hander against the worse hand only while the character is dual wielding, against an equipped two-hander alone; an off-hand or shield with a two-hander equipped is not comparable.
  - **How much.** `score = Σ weight × value` with the weights of the declared spec, `upgrade % = (new − equipped) / equipped`, and the item-level delta. The client's stat keys (`ITEM_MOD_*_SHORT`, `RESISTANCE0_NAME`) map to short codes (`STR`, `SP`, `DPS`, `ARMOR`…).
  - **Verdicts.** *Big* (≥ 5%, preset), *small* (> 0), *none*, *not comparable* (a use-effect trinket, a shirt, an off-hand under a two-hander, no item level); the thresholds are configurable and the item-level comparison has its own (preset 5%).
  - **Reversible default of issue #2.** Default weights for every spec of `Data/Specs.lua` (main stat = 1.0, weapon DPS for melee and hunters), a rough approximation that personal weights and the optional import of M5-3 will replace; nothing decides the open question 8.
  - **Item-level fallback (plan B of spike S5).** Item level only when `useStats` is off, when the stats of either side are unknown, when the spec has no weights or when neither side has a weighted stat; each case leaves a note code.
  - Codes and numbers only: the words arrive with the popup's verdict chip (M5-2). The rules section that will hold the thresholds is not wired yet.
  - Tests: `Tests/test_upgrade_score.lua` (57 tests, including a table of known item pairs for every class).
  - **Not verified in the game:** nothing of this step runs against the client (the inputs come from M5-2); whether the stat keys of `C_Item.GetItemStats` on WoW Forever are the ones mapped here, and whether the proficiency table matches Forever's classes (spike S5 / S6), stay open.

- M4-7: delivery. New `Adapters/MasterLoot.lua` and a delivery part in `App/SessionController.lua`:
  - **Master loot (plan A).** **Confirm and assign** charges the credits and, in the same click, asks the game to hand the item over (`GiveMasterLoot`, through `Compat.Loot`): the item is found again in the open loot window by its id (the slot seen at collection is only a hint) and the winner by full name in the master loot list. Loot messages tell who holds the item.

Opening a body again retries the items that waited for it; a call the game refused waits for the master looter's own click.

- M4-4: session controller. New `App/SessionController.lua` wires `Domain/SessionFSM.lua` to the game without deciding anything itself:
  - **Start.** A boss kill (`ENCOUNTER_END`) opens a session on the master looter's client only, with the open season and the rules in force.
  - **Inputs.** The corpse (`LOOT_COLLECTED`) becomes `LOOT_OPENED`, and is held during a chat lockdown, an addon restriction or a boss, then collected once it is over. The lockdown state becomes `LOCKDOWN`. The raid and addon check become `SET_RAID`. Rolls come from the roll listener and responses from the protocol. A one-second ticker sends `TICK` for the timers and the auto-award window. It never ticks while hidden rolls wait in the roll listener's buffer, so the timer cannot close a round under them.
  - **Effects.** Every accepted input is published to the raid (`Protocol.Session:Publish`) and to the bus (`SESSION_CHANGED`, `SESSION_DELIVER`, `SESSION_HELD`, `SESSION_STARTED`). Confirming writes `ITEM_AWARDED` with its reason and flag codes.
  - **Persistence.** The session lives in the guild's saved data and is changed in place at every transition, so after a `/reload` or a disconnection `Resume` hosts it again and re-attaches the roll listener. A session written by a newer addon is resumed read-only.
  - **Officers.** Other officers get the raid's view read-only (`View`).
  - **Next boss.** A new kill ends the previous session when no item is in progress, and is refused (`SESSION_RUNNING`) while one is.

- M1-6: codec and guarded transport. `Net/Codec.lua` (adapted from Azeroth Accord): the text `<version>|<TYPE>|<payload>` with the payload as CBOR, deflated when that is shorter, in Base64, all through `C_EncodingUtil` via Compat; the version and the type are plain text, so a newer version or an unknown type is ignored without decoding (`PROTOCOL_NEWER` once per session), `Decode` never raises and returns a reason, required fields per type (`Codec:RegisterType`: the session protocol of M3 and the sync of M5 add their types; M1-6 only knows `HELLO`). The prefix carries the protocol version: `FDCP1`. `Net/Transport.lua` (adapted from Accord) sends over `RAID`, `GUILD`, `OFFICER` and `WHISPER` through ChatThrottleLib with the AceComm framing: priorities (ALERT, NORMAL, BULK), one message per gap on each distribution, a rate limit per type (`HELLO`: one per five minutes), the latest message per key wins, retries after a throttle result, and a queue that **waits** during a chat lockdown, an addon restriction **and a boss encounter** (`IsEncounterInProgress`), while the client refusing with a lockdown code is a wait, not a failure. A `RAID` message also waits while the character is not in a raid and is dropped after 120 seconds (a stale session message never reaches the next raid). Every message carries the guild key it was written for (`Config:GuildKey()`): it waits while the guild is unknown and is dropped if the character is in another guild by then. The waiting messages are saved in `FinlandDragonCreditPointsCharDB.outbox` at `PLAYER_LOGOUT` and queued again at `PLAYER_LOGIN` (without callbacks; expired, too old or damaged entries are dropped), so they survive `/reload`. The client's echo of the sender's own `RAID`, `GUILD` and `OFFICER` messages is counted and ignored; the first message or echo that arrives turns the feature `addonMessages` from `unknown` to `available` (spike S4). Incoming messages reach the rest of the addon only as the bus event `NET_MESSAGE(msg, sender, dist)`; sender names are never printed, stored or logged. `/fdcp debug` now also prints the transport and codec counters (any module can add lines with `FDCP:AddDebugSection`; English and Italian keys). `Tests/test_codec.lua` and `Tests/test_transport.lua` cover round trips, every malformed input, pacing, lockdown, restriction and encounter hold, keys, expiry, retries, the guild stamp, `/reload` and the saved queue, the receive path and the real ChatThrottleLib wire with multipart messages replayed to a second receiver.
