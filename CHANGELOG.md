## [Unreleased]

### Added

- M3-5: the addon presence protocol. `Net/Protocol.lua` adds the first session-protocol messages on `RAID`: `HELLOQ{w?, av, pv, pm}` (the `HELLO?` of the analysis: a codec type name is letters and digits only) asks who runs the addon, everybody or only the members named in `w` (at most 40), and carries the asker's own version; `HELLO{av, pv, pm}` answers with the addon version (the TOC `## Version`, `dev` for an unpackaged checkout), the session protocol version and the oldest one the client still understands. Two clients are compatible when each one's `pv` is at least the other's `pm`; a presence message from another channel, or a damaged one, is ignored. Every client says `HELLO` by itself when it enters a raid and after a login or `/reload` in one; the transport's rate for `HELLO` becomes four a minute (was one every five minutes, too few to answer the questions of a night) and `HELLOQ` gets the same. `App/AddonCheck.lua` keeps on every client the same table, since every question and answer travels on `RAID`: the states **active** (compatible, same or newer version), **outdated** (compatible, older: rolls and is asked to update), **incompatible** (cannot roll from the addon), **not active** (asked and no answer within 15 seconds; not installed and disabled look the same) and **waiting** (offline, not asked yet, or asked a moment ago; checked again by itself). An answer counts only if it is not older than the last question that named the player, so a stale answer does not keep someone active after they disable the addon. The runner of the open night asks the whole raid when the night opens (or when they join a raid during it) and only the newcomers at every later join (a member back online counts as one); anyone with `raid.addonCheck` asks again with `AddonCheck:Request()`. A question held by a boss fight or the chat lockdown starts its 15 seconds only when it really leaves. The summary "37/40 with the addon" (English and Italian), one row per raid member for the panel of M3-6, the bus event `ADDON_CHECK_CHANGED`, a `/fdcp debug` line with counts only, and the rule for M4: `AddonCheck:Candidates()` gives `{[name] = true}` for the members with a compatible addon and `AddonCheck:RaidInput()` the `{present, addon}` that `SessionFSM` `SET_RAID` takes. `Tests/sim/sim.lua` is the network simulator the harness already expected: several whole clients in one Lua state, their clocks moved together, `RAID`, `GUILD`, `OFFICER` and `WHISPER` messages carried between them, players without the addon as raid members without a client; the mock raid roster now has an `online` flag. `Tests/test_addon_check.lua` runs simulated raids with an old version, an incompatible protocol, a missing addon, an offline member, a newcomer, a disabled addon, an install and login, a `/reload` and a boss fight.

- M3-4: bench rotation and the calendar. `Domain/Rotation.lua` (pure Lua, on top of `Roster` and `Nights`) proposes who to bench and the master looter decides (`Rotation.Propose` writes nothing): the candidates are the pool's playing mains of the night's faction minus who declined on the calendar and who declared the night's weekday out (`Rotation.Weekday`, the roster's `avail`); the surplus over the raid size sits out in this order: volunteers, calendar "maybe" answers, **fewest benches in the season** (a bench is a night spent on the bench and never played, the planned night not counted, so asking again gives the same answer), most nights played, then the name, so every client proposes the same; a tank or a healer is skipped when benching them would leave the raid under `bench.minTanks` / `bench.minHealers` (capped by who exists), and when the places cannot be filled otherwise the minimum gives way with a warning (`ROLE_TANK`, `ROLE_HEALER`, `TOO_MANY_KEPT`); members the master looter keeps are never proposed and reasons are codes (`VOLUNTEER`, `MAYBE`, `ROTATION`). The `bench` rules grow `creditAmount`, `minTanks` (2) and `minHealers` (4) and `bench.creditEffect` takes `bonus` and `malus` besides `none` (the preset: the rotation never penalizes): `Rotation.CreditEffect` / `Rotation.Credits` add the effect to the ledger's credits for the guilds that choose it (a bonus lowers the credits of a benched member, never below zero; the fewest credits win). `Adapters/Calendar.lua` reads the official raid event of a day through `Compat.Calendar` (raid-type guild, player or community events, the guild's first), the sign-ups of an event (yes, maybe, no) and asks the client to load the calendar at login; `NIGHT_OPENED` takes an optional `eventID` (a number, never a title) and `NightController:Open` links the night to today's raid event; `NightController:ProposeBench` and `ApplyBench` plan and write the bench (one lineup change). Tested offline in `Tests/test_rotation.lua` (a simulated season of 24 nights: the benches differ by at most one inside every role and the minimums hold every night) and `Tests/test_calendar.lua`.

- M3-2: season pool, declarations, mains and alts. `Domain/Roster.lua` (pure Lua, on top of `Members` and `Season`, nothing duplicated) adds the player's **declaration** to the pool: `Roster.Join` writes the usual `MEMBER_JOINED` with `class`, `role`, `ms1` (required: `MS1_REQUIRED`), `ms2`, `os`, `status` (`ACTIVE` | `TRIAL` | `INACTIVE`), `avail` (weekdays `MON`..`SUN`), `note` (at most 80 characters, no sentences of the addon) and `twin`; `Members.Join` only gained the `decl` it copies into the event, and a member written without a declaration is still valid and shows as undeclared. **MS1 = main spec and MS2 = off-spec for the loot**; the third spec (`os`) is roster information and gives no right to roll. The declaration is locked for the season: a second join answers `ALREADY_JOINED` and the only way to change it is `Roster.Change`, an officer's `ROSTER_CHANGED{member, set?, clear?, reason?}` (permission `roster.manage`: a raider's event is refused by the gate, also in a merge), which writes only what really changes (`UNCHANGED` otherwise), never clears the MS1 and checks the corrected declaration as a whole; `Roster.SetStatus` moves a member between active, trial and inactive (an inactive member keeps the credits and is not counted or exported). `Roster.SpecInfo` is the hook for the fixed spec table of M3-3b: with it the class and role come from the MS1 and every spec must belong to the class (`SPEC_UNKNOWN`, `SPEC_CLASS`, `ROLE_MISMATCH`), without it class and role are declared (`CLASS_REQUIRED`, `ROLE_REQUIRED`); the same spec twice is `DUPLICATE_SPEC`. `Roster.State` / `Get` / `List` / `Counts` / `SitePlayers` read the pool (class, spec, role, faction and the site's `Raider` / `Trial` rank for the export), folding the first `MEMBER_JOINED` and the officers' corrections in canonical order, so any merge order gives the same pool. Alts: `Roster.LinkAlt` is `Members.LinkAlt`, an alt is never listed and never has credits (`Roster.CreditHolder` sends it to its main; an alt cannot join as a main and a main cannot become an alt). One main per faction: a player on both factions declares the other main as `twin` (the other faction, both in the pool, one pair per main: `TWIN_FACTION`, `TWIN_TAKEN`, `TWIN_UNKNOWN`, `TWIN_SELF`, `FACTION_REQUIRED`), the credits stay separate, and two claims on the same main resolve to the one that joined first. **Requests**: because the ledger accepts roster events only from `roster.manage`, a player *asks* (`Roster.RequestJoin`, `Roster.RequestAlt`, plain data in a requests table the service will save and the transport of M6 will carry) and an officer *approves* (`Roster.Approve` writes `MEMBER_JOINED` or `MEMBER_LINKED_ALT` through the gate, so a raider cannot approve and the request stays pending) or rejects (`Roster.Reject`); a player's new request replaces his own, another player's gets `REQUEST_PENDING`, at most 500 wait (`REQUEST_LIMIT`), `Roster.Receive` stores a request that came from the network once, `Roster.Prune` drops the old ones. `Roster.ParseNote(note, specs, opts)` is the optional **import from the guild note**: it reads the specs named in the note in order as MS1 / MS2 / OS, a class word, `tank` / `healer` / `dps`, `trial` / `prova`, `inactive` / `inattivo`, resolves a name shared by two classes (Holy) with the class and lists it as `ambiguous` otherwise; it only returns a suggestion for an officer to confirm. The ledger now checks `MEMBER_JOINED`'s declaration fields and `ROSTER_CHANGED`. `Domain/Reasons.lua` has 16 new refusal codes and a `STATUS` group, each with an English and an Italian sentence (also in the **Loot Rules** wiki page, both languages). Choices made where the analysis was silent (`docs/ANALISI.md` §6, "Come è fatto (M3-2)"): classes limited to the site's nine, roles are the client's `TANK` / `HEALER` / `DAMAGER`, the faction is fixed by the join and `ROSTER_CHANGED` cannot correct it (a wrong faction waits for a later step), a note is player data and not a sentence of the addon, "one main per faction" is enforced through the declared `twin` because the addon cannot know which characters are the same person. `Tests/test_roster.lua` covers the declaration and its checks, the lock and the gate, corrections, statuses, alts, the twin rule, the requests, merge convergence and the note import.

- M2-6: the session state machine. `Domain/SessionFSM.lua` runs a loot session of `docs/ANALISI.md` §4 as one plain-data table saved in the SavedVariables, so a `/reload` resumes it identically (`SessionFSM.Restore`): `SessionFSM.Step(session, input, env)` applies one input and the automatic phases and returns effects for the adapters (announce, open the popup, `GiveMasterLoot`, the "no addon" warning); a refused input changes nothing. States `WAITING` (lockdown or boss) → `COLLECTING` (items above the threshold, priced; the ML corrects them with a reason) → `ANNOUNCING` → `ROLLING` (the first round also collects the responses) → `COMPUTING` (`Priority.Evaluate` asks for a reroll among the tied, the next tier or a free roll) → `PROPOSAL` → confirm (writes `ITEM_AWARDED` with the reason codes through the trust gate) / reject (`PENDING`, `REOPEN`, `NEXT_TIER`, `BANK`, `DISENCHANT`) / modify (mandatory reason code; flags `OUT_OF_ORDER`, `COST_CHANGED`, `ML_DECIDED`; the rule's winner and cost kept in the event) → `ASSIGNED` → `DELIVERED` (also after `END`, for a delayed trade). Pause keeps the remaining time of the timers; `session.autoAdvance` closes a round on its timer or once everyone with the addon has answered and rolled and opens rerolls, tiers and the next item; `session.awardMode` `confirm`, `auto` (only "ready" proposals, after `interventionSeconds`, never after an intervention) and `council` (the ML closes a vote, a strict majority decides). One item at a time; the raid, the addon list and the credits are frozen on the item when its roll opens, so a player who enables the addon comes back from the next item and a roll from a player without it is discarded as `NO_ADDON` (§4.1). New rule `session.rollSeconds` (30 s). The domain never reads the clock: inputs carry the time and `TICK` checks the deadlines. Tests: `Tests/test_session_fsm.lua`.

- M2-5: rules, presets and rank permissions. `Domain/Rules.lua` is the configuration schema of `docs/ANALISI.md` §3 in sections (`night`, `members`, `season`, `cost`, `priority`, `session`, `council`, `legendary`, `bench`; the sections of M2-1 to M2-4 keep their own defaults and checks), with `Rules.Validate` (sorted `section.field` codes), `Rules.WithDefaults` (fills a missing section or key, never inside a key that is present) and `Rules.Diff` (the changed paths, for the audit); the rules live in the ledger as `CONFIG_CHANGED{rules, preset?, confirmed?}` carrying the whole validated configuration, and `Rules.Current(L, at)` gives the rules in force when an event was written. `Domain/Presets.lua`: `PATTO` (the defaults, §3.1), `ROLL_ONLY` ("Solo roll": highest roll, nothing costs credits), `LOOT_COUNCIL` ("Loot council puro": the council votes, nothing costs credits), `EPGP_SIMPLE` ("EPGP semplice": fewest credits per night played); `Presets.Match` and `Presets.Apply`. `Domain/Permissions.lua`: permission → lowest guild rank **index** (0 = GM), `config.rules` and `config.permissions` fixed at the GM, preset `season.manage` and `credits.adjust` for the GM and the rest for rank index 1 (officers), the table as `PERMISSIONS_CHANGED{table, confirmed?}`, one permission per ledger type (voids and manual corrections need `credits.adjust` outside a night, `night.run` in one). `Ledger.Append` and `Ledger.Merge` take a trust gate (`Permissions.Gate`): an event is accepted only if its author had the required rank when it was written, under the table in force then (a merge batch is applied in canonical order), a config event only from the GM (`NOT_GM`); otherwise `RANK_TOO_LOW` / `NOT_IN_GUILD`, or `PENDING` while the guild roster is not loaded (returned for the caller to queue). The rank at a time comes from a rank history (`RecordRank` / `RankAt`) the M3-1 adapter will keep, so a GM change hands over the config power and the old GM's changes stay valid; ranks are indexes, so renaming a rank changes nothing, and `RanksChanged` / `CheckRanks` report reordered, removed or added ranks to the GM. Rules and permission changes while a season is open need an explicit `confirmed` flag (`CONFIRM_REQUIRED`), kept in the event for the audit. `Season.Open/Close` and `Members.Join/LinkAlt` pass the gate on. Tests: `Tests/test_rules.lua`, `Tests/test_permissions.lua`.

- M2-4: seasons, members and late joiners. `Domain/Season.lua` reads seasons from the ledger: `Season.Open` writes `SEASON_OPENED{label?, instances = {{id, size}}, startCredits}` (the pack of instances, each 10, 20 or 40; `startCredits` from the configuration, preset 0, frozen in the event so a later configuration change never touches an open season), `Season.Close` writes `SEASON_CLOSED`; one season open at a time and an id is never reused (`SEASON_ALREADY_OPEN`, `SEASON_EXISTS`); after a merge the first opening of an id in canonical order counts and a close counts even when the officer's clock put it before the opening. `Season.CreditTable(season, instanceID, difficulty)` gives the season's one credit table for every instance of the pack, every size and both Normal and Veteran (`NOT_IN_PACK`, `DIFFICULTY` otherwise); `Season.NightCount` counts `NIGHT_OPENED`. `Domain/Members.lua`: `Members.Join` writes `MEMBER_JOINED{member, faction?, initialCredits, rule, basis?}`, `Members.LinkAlt` writes `MEMBER_LINKED_ALT{alt, main, faction?}`; only the declared main holds credits (`Members.Resolve` sends an alt to its main), a main is never an alt, a main and its alts share one faction, so a player on both factions has two mains with separate credits (`IS_ALT`, `ALT_IS_MAIN`, `ALREADY_LINKED`, `MAIN_UNKNOWN`, `FACTION_MISMATCH`); after a merge the first join and the first link count and a member wins over a link. Late joiners (`docs/ANALISI.md` §3.5): after the season's first night the initial credits are `average` (preset) | `median` | `min` | `zero` | `fixed` of the basis "members with at least N nights" (preset 1; the nights come from the lineup of M3, without them the whole pool), rounded `floor` (preset) | `round` | `ceil` to 0-2 decimals, written once in `MEMBER_JOINED` with the rule code (`START`, `AVERAGE`, `MEDIAN`, `MIN`, `ZERO`, `FIXED`, `EMPTY_BASIS`) and the basis size, so the value stays the same when the average moves, after voids, snapshots and merges. `Members.Roster` lists the pool by credits. The ledger now checks the payloads of `SEASON_OPENED`, `MEMBER_JOINED` (faction `Horde` / `Alliance`, rule code, basis) and `MEMBER_LINKED_ALT`. - M2-3: the event ledger. `Domain/Ledger.lua` keeps immutable events `{id, author, seq, ts, season, type, v, payload}`; the id is `author:seq`, unique per author, so a merge is a set union. `Ledger.Append` writes the author's next event (a void must reference an existing, voidable, not yet voided event), `Ledger.Merge` takes events in any order and any number of times (duplicates change nothing, a different event with the same id is reported as a conflict and the stored one wins, malformed events are refused with a code). Every event type of `docs/ANALISI.md` §8.3 is known, plus `SEASON_CLOSED`, `PROGRESS_SET`, `PERMISSIONS_CHANGED` and `LEGENDARY_PROJECT_OPENED` / `_PAID` / `_CLOSED`; payloads hold codes and numbers (reasons are codes like `WRONG_WINNER`, never sentences). Corrections are compensation, never deletion: `AWARD_VOIDED{ref, reason}` cancels an `ITEM_AWARDED` or a `CREDITS_ADJUSTED` and restores the previous credits, two voids of the same award count once, a void that arrives before its award still cancels it. Credits are computed: the first `MEMBER_JOINED{initialCredits}` of the member (canonical order: time, author, sequence) plus the costs of the awards to the member plus the adjustments, minus what is voided, rounded to 2 decimals; bank and disenchant awards move nothing. Snapshots (`Ledger.TakeSnapshot`, newest three kept) cover exactly the events up to the per-author high-water marks, so snapshot + tail always equals the full ledger, even when older events arrive later. For officer sync (M6): `Ledger.Heads` (contiguous high-water mark per author), `Ledger.Since(heads)` (what a peer lacks), `Ledger.Gaps`. Payload schema versions: older payloads are upgraded on read through `Ledger.UPGRADES` (the stored event never changes); an unknown type or a newer version is kept and forwarded for sync but ignored in the totals; a ledger table written by a newer addon opens read-only. `Ledger.FirstKills(faction)` gives the oldest date per encounter from `ENCOUNTER_KILLED` and `PROGRESS_SET` for the Progress export. `Tests/test_ledger.lua` covers append, merge, voids, adjustments, conflicts, versions, the sync helpers, snapshots and 25 random event sequences from three officers checked against an independent model (shuffled, batched, duplicated, snapshotted on the way).
